Help

Estimate a length on a flat surface. Place the printed reference card beside the item and take a photo with your normal camera.

Measure the marker first

Use the card’s actual outer black-square side, not its nominal print size. The workspace corrects perspective from its four corners; tap the two ends of the length you want.

Keep the card and measurement in the same plane. Raised objects, blur and extreme angles can introduce error. Results are estimates, not precision measurements.

Enable Allow photo measurement in Module access. Photo selection, marker detection and measurements stay native and local. Nothing is saved or shared with this module. Rotation keeps your current work. Leaving closes the workspace and clears unsaved work.

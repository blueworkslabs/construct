Pocket Camera

A quiet place to take a photo. Open the viewfinder, frame your shot, then press Take photo.

Ready. The camera stays off until you open it.

Private by default

Photos start in this module's private album. On supported hosts, you can explicitly save a separate copy to your phone gallery from the native workspace. Module code never receives images or live frames, and cannot export them.

Up to 8 private photos / 20 MiB. Gallery copies are independent and may be read or backed up by your photo apps. Closing, backgrounding or rotating closes the camera.

Both access gates must be on: Module access → Allow camera workspace, then Allow Android camera access. You can delete retained photos there even when access is off.

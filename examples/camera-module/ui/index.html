Pocket Camera

A quiet place to take a photo. Open the viewfinder, frame your shot, then press Take photo.

Ready. The camera stays off until you open it.

Private by design

Photos stay in this module's private album, not your phone gallery. No microphone, upload or export. Module code never receives images or live frames.

Up to 8 photos / 20 MiB. View or delete them in the native workspace. Closing, backgrounding or rotating closes the camera.

Both access gates must be on: Module access → Allow camera workspace, then Allow Android camera access. You can delete retained photos there even when access is off.

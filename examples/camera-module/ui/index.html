    Privacy and limits

    The native viewfinder shows live camera video. Only its shutter takes a photo; live frames never reach this module. Face and object detection runs on a selected still image, not live video.

    This module has no network capability. Its private album holds up to 8 photos / 20 MiB. Gallery copies need your confirmation and may be accessed or backed up by other photo apps. Closing the module clears displayed pixels and analysis, but keeps private originals.

    Detection is an estimate and can miss things. Face detection does not identify people or infer emotions. No results are saved into your photo.

    Enable the image, private-photo, capture and local-analysis grants in Module access as needed. Android camera permission is separate.

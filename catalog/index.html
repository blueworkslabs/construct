Small tools. Your device.

Construct

Install small, signed modules. Choose their access. Keep your data on your phone.

Get the Android app

Download alpha21 from GitHub Releases

An early-access build for ARM64 Android phones. Release notes include checksums and known limitations.

Connect the catalog

Open the catalog index, copy its full HTTPS address, and enter it as the catalog URL in Construct’s Settings. Save it, then refresh Browse.

Already installed? Changing the catalog address does not require reinstalling the app.

Available modules

Hello Module · Pocket Checklist · Pocket Tones · Pocket Focus · Pocket Snake · Pocket Contacts · Pocket Camera · Pocket Measure

Downloads contain module code, not your personal data. Installed modules remain available offline. Capabilities depend on your app version and the access you grant.

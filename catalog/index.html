Install small, signed modules. Choose their access. Keep your data on your phone.

Get the Android app

Download alpha22 from GitHub Releases

An early-access build for ARM64 Android phones. Release notes include checksums and known limitations.

Connect the catalog

New to alpha22? The public catalog is already selected. Open Browse and tap Refresh catalog.

Upgrading? Install the APK over your existing app to keep your modules and data. Saved catalog choices stay unchanged; to switch, open Settings, tap Use configured registry, then Use catalog.

On an older build, copy the full HTTPS address of the catalog index into Settings, save it, and refresh Browse. No reinstall is needed just to change the catalog.

Available modules

Downloads contain module code, not your personal data. Installed modules remain available offline. Capabilities depend on your app version and the access you grant.
